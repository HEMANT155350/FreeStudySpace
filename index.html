Good to know

A few things
you might wonder.

Are the study notes free?

Yes. You can browse the guides and download each one as a PDF for free.

Can I preview a PDF before downloading?

Yes. Open a resource to preview the PDF in the website, then download it if you want to keep a copy.

Can I use these instead of my course materials?

These guides are a starting point for revision, not a replacement for your class notes, textbook, or course syllabus.

How are new notes and practical files added?

New PDFs are added to the site's PDF folder and listed in its notes catalogue. The library grows as new files are published.
