Good to know

A few things
you might wonder.

Are the study notes free?

Yes. You can browse the guides and download each one as a PDF for free.

How do I download a PDF?

Choose a note and select “Read & download PDF.” The guide is formatted into a PDF and saved by your browser.

Can I use these instead of my course materials?

These guides are a starting point for revision, not a replacement for your class notes, textbook, or course syllabus.

What if I can't find the topic I need?

Try a different keyword or subject filter. The library is a starting collection, and not every topic is covered yet.
