Good to know

A few things
you might wonder.

What is FreeStudySpace?

FreeStudySpace is here to help people discover places where they can study, work, and focus without paying for a seat.

Are all study spaces really free?

We focus on spaces that are free to access, but opening hours, entry rules, and amenities can vary. Check with a location before visiting.

Do I need to be a student?

No. Everyone deserves a good place to focus, whether you're studying, working, or learning something new.

Can I just show up?

Some places welcome drop-ins, while others may have access requirements or limited hours. It's always a good idea to check first.
